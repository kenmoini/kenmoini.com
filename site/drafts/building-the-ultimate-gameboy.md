> We are all children in Nintendo's eyes

So growing up I never really had my own video game consoles or handhelds - our family simply didn't have the means, and that's ok...*because now I have a considerable amount of expendable income to get all the things I wanted as a kid!*

In high school I ran a little side hustle with some friends where we bought broken consoles and phones on eBay and then fixed them up, sold them back at a profit - it was a great gig.  We'd also mod consoles, sometimes being required to fix the issues - this was back in the hay day of those consoles such as the PS2, GameCube, and original Xbox.

I had been chatting with a coworker and found out he had a similar stint in that scene, and it got me thinking...*what's the console modding scene look like today?*

Well, while you can't find the golden Xecutor CE 3 modchips any more, the console modding scene in general is more alive than it ever was.
