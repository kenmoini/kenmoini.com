I have a **problem** - *ok, I have a lot of problems*...but the one I'm talking about is mostly due to a bad compulsive shopping habit.  When paired with nostalgia sprung from YouTube rabbit holes, I end up buying a bunch of old computers.

It started off harmless...an iMac here...a Handspring there...but then before I knew it I had most of the vintage computers from my past sitting next to me in my den with swelling capacitors waiting to be replaced.

I was brought up on some of this old stuff...which is a pretty lucky thing for me, cause having been a 90s baby I shouldn't have really been exposed to things like systems with no internal hard drive that could only boot MS-DOS 3 off one 5.25" floppy and then utilize the other 5.25" floppy drive to load other programs.  Learning all this old stuff really gave me an interesting vantage point most of my generation never got, and I'm hoping aside from the stroll down Memory Lane that my daughter will find some interest in this history as well...so far she really likes smashing on all the keyboards and flipping the big thud'ing switches.  She did jump over me to get to the Compaq Portable as soon as I unboxed it, so fingers crossed I guess heh.

I won't go listing all the vintage gear I got and all the things I'm going to do with them - but to support them I'm going to need to do a few things here at the house and in the homelab.  A lot of which was inspired by this guy on YouTube by the username of [clabretro](https://www.youtube.com/@clabretro).  I blame a lot of this on him and some other YouTubers.

Now I distinctly remember having to compress and split files to fit on a floppy disk, which sucked - hell, I remember transporting low grade smut on floppy disks from one computer to another.  I am not about to try to track down working floppies just to move `XXX-hot-Britney-Spears-nude-naked-sexy-real-totallyNotFake.bmp` around again.

No, because this is the 21st century by dammit, in the year of our lord 2025 smut will be streamed over the airwaves!  Or at least over the telephone lines that is.
